A macro is a group of instructions that perform one task like a procedure, and insert a block of statements at various points of usage in the program during assembly.

**Uses of macro:**

-> To simplify and reduce the amount of repetitive coding
-> To reduce errors caused by repetitive coding
-> To stream line an assembly language program to make it more reliable

### Repetition

The repetition directives REPT,IRP and IRPC cause the assembler to repeat a block of statements up to the directive’s terminating ENDM statement.
