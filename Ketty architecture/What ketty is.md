
ketty is a Social Portfolio and Hobby Tacker Web App. Through ketty, users can track their hobbies, projects and anything they want to track. Apps like Spotify, letterboxd, myanimelist have features to track a person's favourite anime, music, movies. With ketty, users can track all of them at once. It doesn't have every feature that these apps do, it is simply a way for people to track their favourite things. It serves like a personal record of everything an individual has an interest in. Users can also track what they've done in life, like projects. With a projects section, I can easily display what I'm working on as well as track my progress. I have always wanted a place where I could track everything I've ever done, things I like. Users can also post pictures or videos. Users can post under 'topics'. For example, someone can posts under robotics, wood cutting, or whatever they like. Topics serve as an organisation method, because I've noticed posts on Instagram, Facebook are scattered, so I thought it'd be better if we could organise our posts. Another simple feature which my app allows is GIFs as profile pics.
