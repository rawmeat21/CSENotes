

**1. Supervised Learning**  
Model learns from labeled input-output pairs. Two subtypes:

- _Classification_ — predict a category (spam/not spam)
- _Regression_ — predict a continuous value (house price)

**2. Unsupervised Learning**  
No labels at all — model finds structure on its own. Includes:

- _Clustering_ (grouping similar points, e.g. customer segments)
- _Dimensionality reduction_ (e.g. PCA)
- _Anomaly detection_

**3. Semi-supervised Learning**  
Small amount of labeled data + large amount of unlabeled data. Common when labeling is expensive (e.g. labeling X-rays).

**4. Reinforcement Learning**  
An agent learns by interacting with an environment, getting rewards/penalties — no fixed dataset, learning happens through trial and error.
